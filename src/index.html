How the score is calculated

A simple average of relevant daily progress measures.

The score averages available daily progress measures: task completion rate when tasks were created today, focus progress toward 4 sessions when there is activity, and scheduled activity completion when activities are scheduled today. This is a general progress indicator, not a scientific measure.
